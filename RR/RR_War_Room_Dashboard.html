Model Architecture: Why This Ranking Repeats Across Seasons

Weights derived from out-of-sample split-half reliability optimization

1. Expected Pressure Value 75% Weight

Overall baseline skill (K₀=60) plus an Empirical-Bayes shrunk pressure deviation:
Score = Base + n/(n+400) × (Pressure RV - Base) Prevents small-sample flukes from topping the table.

2. Pressure Rating (PR) 15% Weight

Win-Probability Conversion: Sum of Win Probability Added (WPA) divided by sum of available Leverage Index (LI) faced by the player, shrunk with half a median player's leverage mass.

3. Consistency & Floor 10% Weight

Combination of positive impact rate (% of pressure innings with RV ≥ 0, shrunk to 50% with 8 pseudo-innings) and the 25th-percentile "bad day floor" to protect RR against volatility.
